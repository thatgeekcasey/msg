Ember 0.5 brings huddles, AI agents that use other services' apps, Slack's composer, and more ways to keep a community safe, which fifteen betas tried first. Here's what's new, what updating from 0.4 does, and every pull request since 0.4.2.

## What's new

### Huddles

Call a room or a DM by voice, camera and shared screen, laid out as Slack's huddles are, for up to 50 people, over [Cloudflare Realtime SFU](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/sfu/). A huddle in a DM rings the others in it, **Invite people** rings whoever else you pick, and whoever doesn't have Ember in view gets the invitation as a push notification. Video is HD unless an administrator picks Standard or Full HD, and steps down on a slow connection, with a warning when one is unstable. Raise a hand, show a sticker, or react with emoji. Each huddle has a message in its room, whose thread the huddle shows beside the people in it, and **Huddles**, in the sidebar, lists those going on and the recent ones. Each person chooses which of a huddle's sounds they hear.

An administrator turns huddles on in Admin's Huddles tab with a Realtime SFU app's keys, can give them a relay for networks that block calls, can turn cameras or screen sharing off, and can turn huddles off in a room, in its settings. [Huddles](https://github.com/NYLLON-SOFTWARE/ember-cloudflare/blob/v0.5.0/docs/self-hosting.md#huddles) says how, and [What it costs](https://github.com/NYLLON-SOFTWARE/ember-cloudflare/blob/v0.5.0/docs/self-hosting.md#what-it-costs) what they cost.

### Agents, apps and outside AI

- **Agents of your own.** Beside [at]ember, administrators make agents, each with its instructions, model and rooms, which answer when they're mentioned or written to. **Available Agents**, in the sidebar, lists those each person can ask, and an administrator can keep one for administrators.
- **Claude, GPT and Grok** through AI Gateway, and Workers AI's models, from lists every copy reads each day, so new ones come without an update. Each is asked at only the Thinking levels it takes, and Grok searches the web with Ember's own tool.
- **Apps.** An administrator connects another service's MCP server, signing in to it where it asks, and picks which of its tools each agent may use. An agent calls a tool once someone allows the call on its card, which only whoever asked sees, unless an administrator always allows that tool in open rooms. A tool can answer with a card whose buttons call it.
- **Reading the room.** Agents read the room before a thread, read back through the room and its threads, and search by who wrote what, but only in the conversation they're asked in. Asked for a card, they answer with one.
- **Outside AI.** An AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT can connect to Ember as one of its people, over MCP, to read rooms and threads, search, and send messages as them when they allow it, in their DMs and private rooms only if they let it in.
- Administrators can change what Ember adds around the instructions, and an answer that AI Gateway's Guardrails or DLP blocked says so.

### Slack's composer

Slack's formatting row over the text and its row of buttons under it, / commands, video and audio clips, drafts to come back to, and messages scheduled for later. Quote line by line, copy a code block with the button in its corner, read a text file as a snippet in its message, and write up to 40,000 characters.

### Mentions and notifications

- [at]channel and [at]here notify a room, and [at]everyone an open one, asking first in a room of six or more. Administrators can keep them to themselves, or turn them off in a room. A bot's `<!here>`, `<!channel>` and `<!everyone>` notify as they do in Slack.
- Mention a room with #. Someone who isn't in a private room sees a lock and "private room" in place of its name.
- An unread room's row counts the mentions waiting in it, and **Pause notifications** quiets them all for a while.

### Getting around

- A top bar, as Slack's, with search, History, and Back and Forward.
- Named sections in the sidebar, which fold. New rooms go at the bottom, and DMs lists only the DMs you're in.
- Whoever joins has a DM with each person already here, which opens with their joining, and DM rows show who's active and how many messages wait.
- A New line above what came while a room was unread, and the keyboard shortcuts in a dialog, on ⌘/ or Ctrl+/.
- A link's preview joins its message a moment after Send.

### Membership and safety

- Single sign-on, through any OpenID Connect provider, such as Zitadel, Keycloak, Okta, Entra ID or Google.
- Memberful set up in Admin, its keys kept sealed, with a welcome for new members, who choose their photo, name and username.
- **Delete my account** on the profile, **Block** someone, **Report** a message or someone, and **Blocked words** in Membership.
- Lifting a ban puts the person back in every open room, and a member can leave a private room by themselves.
- Fixes for what the security reviews of 13 and 19 November found. The search index keeps private rooms' and DMs' words apart, under keys of their own, and the API refuses other sites' reads, as it did their writes.

### Bots

Bots read threads with `?thread=`, are given the files people post, have a webhook key of their own, answer in the thread they were asked in, and hear clicks on their cards' buttons, to put a new card in its place. Atlassian Statuspage's webhooks show as cards, and the Chat bots page folds each bot's rooms and addresses away.

### Updates

- General's Ember card comes first, with **Check for updates**, and a tab left open across an update says **Ember has been updated**, with **Reload**.
- A copy takes the next release's betas only when it asks, with Update Ember's **Take Ember's newest beta** box or the beta workflow that General offers while one is out.
- Cloudflare Images makes a picture's preview where its uploader sent none, as bots don't.

## Updating from 0.4

Your copy's Update Ember workflow offers it as a pull request, "Update Ember to v0.5.0", at its next weekly run, or now if you run it from the Actions tab. General's Ember card links to the workflow, or, for a copy without it, to adding it. Merging the pull request deploys 0.5, and the rest happens by itself:

- Each room's and the workspace's tables bring themselves up to date the first time they open, with 39 new migrations and 3 of the search index's, which add to the tables and take nothing away.
- The update's `wrangler.jsonc` adds two Durable Object classes, AppConnector and OutsideAi, and the Images binding, which need nothing made first, and keeps your Worker's and bucket's names.
- It needs no new secret. Everything new that needs setting up, such as huddles, agents, apps, outside AI and single sign-on, stays off until an administrator turns it on.
- Ember AI, if it's on, goes on answering with the models it had. Claude and GPT take a token with AI Gateway Run, which the one Ember AI was set up with on 0.4 may lack, so to offer them, set Ember AI up again with a token that has the permissions it lists, as [Ember AI](https://github.com/NYLLON-SOFTWARE/ember-cloudflare/blob/v0.5.0/docs/self-hosting.md#ember-ai) says.
- Update Ember's own workflow has a new version, with the box for betas, and the pull request says so, for you to copy it over yours.

A copy on a 0.5 beta is offered this release as its next update, and releases alone from then on.
